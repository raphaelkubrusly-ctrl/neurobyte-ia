neurobyte_ia Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

Welcome to neurobyte_ia. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our platform and its TikTok integration.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account to neurobyte_ia, we collect your TikTok open ID, avatar, and display name (user.info.basic) solely to personalize your experience on our platform. We also collect content you choose to upload or post via the Content Posting API (video.upload, video.publish).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to authenticate your identity, display your profile within the app, and enable you to share educational content to your TikTok account as drafts or direct posts. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. TikTok Data

Data obtained through TikTok APIs is used only as authorized by TikTok's developer terms and solely for the purposes described in this policy. We request only the minimum necessary permissions: user.info.basic, video.upload, and video.publish.

4. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Data is stored securely and retained only as long as necessary to provide our services.

5. Your Rights

You may revoke neurobyte_ia's access to your TikTok account at any time through TikTok's privacy settings. You may also contact us to request deletion of any data we hold about you.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our platform.
